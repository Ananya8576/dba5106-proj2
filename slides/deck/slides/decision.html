The blackbox has the highest validation AUC, by 0.006, and the interval only just excludes zero. But at our cost ratio its evaluation loss is not lower: 0.149 against 0.143. AUC averages over all thresholds; our costs only care about the low-threshold end. We had set a rule in advance: choose the blackbox only if it lowers the cost by at least 10 percent. So we chose the glassbox, L1 logistic regression. Then the threshold: the cost-minimising threshold is 0.28, not 0.5. That gives up 1.8 points of accuracy and cuts the cost by 27 percent. This is the evidence that the evaluation loss, not accuracy, should drive the decision.
