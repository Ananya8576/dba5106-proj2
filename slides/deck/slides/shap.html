The brief requires tree SHAP on a tree-based blackbox even though we chose the glassbox. We applied it to the log-loss gradient-boosted trees, on the validation split. Global: the mean absolute SHAP value per feature. The radius of the largest cluster is far ahead at 4.0, then the edge fail rate at 1.8 and the outer ring fail rate at 0.9. The model asks the question an engineer would: is the cluster at the edge? Local: two wafers picked by rule, not by hand. The most confident Edge-Loc: the cluster is at the edge and that one feature carries most of the prediction. The costliest mistake: a wafer labelled Edge-Loc that the model scores as Loc. Its largest cluster is near the centre, and the glassbox agrees with the blackbox, so this may be a labelling error. Cautions: the fail-rate features are correlated, so the ranking among them is not reliable. And the glassbox is not automatically readable either: its coefficients have large opposite signs for the same reason. SHAP values add up exactly to the model's output.
