Calibration: mildly off, then fixed

0.031 → 0.021

calibration error (ECE) of the chosen model; AUC unchanged

What was wrong

It under-predicts between 0.2 and 0.5, where our threshold sits.

The fix

Isotonic regression, fitted on out-of-fold training scores so validation stays clean. Platt scaling barely helps a logistic model (0.029).

Why it matters

With calibrated probabilities, theory gives the threshold: 1/(1+5) = 0.167. Ours is 0.22, at almost the same cost (0.143 vs 0.148).

Validation split, equal-count bins. The glassbox stays the cheaper model for cost ratios from 3 to 8.

We checked calibration with a reliability diagram, the expected calibration error and the Brier score, for all four models. The L2 glassbox is the worst, with an error of 0.107. Our chosen model is mildly off at 0.031: it under-predicts between 0.2 and 0.5, exactly where the threshold sits. The fix: isotonic regression fitted on out-of-fold training scores, so the validation split is not reused. That takes the error to 0.021 and leaves AUC unchanged. Platt scaling barely helps a logistic model, but it works well for the blackbox, 0.034 to 0.013. Why it matters: with calibrated probabilities, decision theory gives the threshold directly as 1 over 1 plus the cost ratio, 0.167. Our searched threshold is 0.22, and using 0.167 costs almost the same. We also varied the cost ratio from 1 to 10: the glassbox stays the cheaper model from 3 to 8.
